When in the day is the air cleanest?

Average of every station's hourly PM since January 2025 as an AQI-equivalent. CPCB's AQI is a 24-hour value; this shows the daily rhythm.

How much does Diwali add?

Each day's AQI minus the average of days 30 to 11 before Diwali. Part of the rise is the normal post-monsoon worsening, so this is an upper bound on the festival's own effect.

How AirAware works

  1. Stations. Readings from 37 CPCB monitors (14 in Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad, 23 in Mumbai), cleaned of error codes, stuck sensors and impossible values, averaged into a 24-hour city AQI.
  2. Weather and CAMS. Temperature, humidity, rain, wind, pressure and mixing height, plus the Copernicus CAMS particle forecast, for today and the target day.
  3. Model. Ridge regression on the change from today's AQI, one model per horizon (1 to 7 days). Tested month by month on data it had never seen.
  4. Areas. A clicked spot gets an inverse-distance-weighted average of nearby stations; its forecast is the city forecast shifted by how that spot compares with the city.

Data: CPCB via the OpenAQ archive, ERA5 and forecasts from Open-Meteo, CAMS from Copernicus, maps © OpenStreetMap. The public archive runs about four days behind; with an OpenAQ key the site switches to live hourly readings.
