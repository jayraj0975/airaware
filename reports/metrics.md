# AirAware results

Generated by `python -m airaware.model`. Walk-forward test: at the start of each month from October 2025, every method is retrained on the rows whose target had already been measured, then forecasts that month. Next day: 662 city-day forecasts for 02 Oct 2025 to 06 Oct 2026; every method is scored on exactly the same rows. Lower MAE/RMSE is better.

**AirAware** (the model the site serves) uses station history plus weather forecasts **as they were issued** (Open-Meteo's archive of past runs; today's weather from the run issued a day earlier, the target day's from runs issued h + 1 days before it), and station history only at 7 days ahead, where no archived run is old enough. **Station history only** uses the stations' AQI, PM2.5 and PM10 (today, the two days before, the 7-day mean), the calendar and the city: no weather and no CAMS at all, so it is the most conservative benchmark. **Upper bound** adds the realized (ERA5) weather and the CAMS analysis for the target day. Nobody has those on the day, and CAMS keeps no archive of its past forecasts, so this row only shows how much better the forecast could be with perfect weather and particle forecasts. It is not an estimate of live accuracy.
